# ADR-008: Monitoring Strategy

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The platform requires availability, performance, and queue health visibility.

## Decision
Collect metrics, traces, health checks, and alerts for API, workers, database, Redis, queues, and notification providers.

## Alternatives
Manual log review, uptime checks only.

## Consequences
Incidents are detected earlier. Teams must maintain dashboards, alert thresholds, and on-call runbooks.
